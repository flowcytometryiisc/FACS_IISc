About the facility

Divisional FACS Facility, Indian Institute of Science

ESTABLISHED 1997

Supporting advanced scientific research

The Divisional FACS Facility at the Indian Institute of Science was established in 1997 with support from the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India.

The current instrument lineup includes four flow cytometers supporting advanced scientific research. All are operator-assisted, and offline analysis systems are available for flow-cytometry users.

User-charge information, research resources, analysis-software listings and workshop information are available through the dedicated pages on this website.
